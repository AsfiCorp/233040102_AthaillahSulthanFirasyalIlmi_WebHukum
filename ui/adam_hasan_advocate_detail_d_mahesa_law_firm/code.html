Adam Hasan SH, S.Pt, MH, ME, CLA, CIL

Senior Partner

Adam Hasan represents the pinnacle of interdisciplinary legal strategy at D'Mahesa. With a rare combination of advanced degrees spanning jurisprudence, engineering, and economics, his approach to complex corporate litigation and regulatory compliance is unmatched in its analytical depth.

As a Senior Partner, Adam spearheads the firm’s high-stakes corporate restructuring and international trade dispute divisions. His unique background allows him to dissect highly technical evidence and translate it into compelling legal narratives, offering clients—ranging from multinational conglomerates to emerging tech innovators—a decisive advantage in the courtroom and at the negotiating table.

His extensive credentials, including certifications as a Certified Legal Auditor (CLA) and Certified International Lawyer (CIL), underscore his commitment to meticulous risk assessment and global legal standards. Adam’s philosophy centers on proactive legal engineering: structuring operations and transactions to preemptively mitigate exposure while maximizing commercial viability.
